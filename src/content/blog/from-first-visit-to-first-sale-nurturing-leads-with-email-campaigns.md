Most visitors to your Shopify store won’t buy on their first visit. They’re browsing, price-checking, or just not ready to make a decision yet.

But just because they’re not buying today doesn’t mean they won’t buy later — especially if you stay in touch.

That’s where **email campaigns** come in. With Uppush, you can capture those visitors, follow up automatically, and turn missed opportunities into actual revenue.

## 🧲 Capture leads before they bounce

The first step is simple: get visitors to subscribe.

Uppush includes customizable popups and signup forms you can add right to your store — no code needed. Whether you’re offering a discount, new product alerts, or just inviting people to stay in the loop, it’s an easy way to start building your list.

Once they’ve subscribed, you’ve got a direct line to their inbox — no algorithms, no ad spend.

## 📧 Send campaigns that convert

Email lets you build trust and turn interest into sales — especially if you keep your messages relevant.

Uppush makes it easy to create and send:

-   New product or collection announcements
-   Time-limited promotions
-   First-time buyer discounts
-   Re-engagement messages for inactive subscribers

You can target specific groups based on behavior, country, purchase history, or custom tags — all without needing to export your data anywhere.

Need inspiration? Start with one of your saved templates or duplicate a past campaign.

## ⚙️ Automate follow-ups with zero friction

You don’t need to build complex flows from scratch — Uppush comes with **pre-built automations** that are already configured and ready to go. You can activate them instantly, or customize timing and content if you want more control.

Here are a few worth turning on:

-   **Abandoned Cart Recovery**  
    Sends web push and 3 emails after a subscriber adds an item to their cart but doesn’t check out.  
    → Sent after: 30 minutes, 2 hours, and 3 days.
-   **Abandoned Checkout Recovery**  
    Triggers if someone starts checkout but doesn’t complete it within 10 minutes.  
    → Sends multiple follow-ups via email and web notifications.
-   **Welcome Series**  
    Greets new subscribers with a short sequence.  
    → Sent immediately, then after 2 hours, and again after 2 days.
-   **Other handy automations**  
    Automatically notify subscribers about:
    -   Back in stock items
    -   Price drops
    -   New product releases

All of these come with pre-made templates you can edit or use as-is.

## 📊 Track what’s working

Every campaign and automation includes performance insights:

-   Open and click rates
-   Conversions and revenue generated
-   Engagement by country or segment

These stats make it easy to test different content, learn what’s working, and improve over time — no guesswork involved.

Some customers need more time — and that’s okay. If you’ve got a system for staying in touch, you’ll catch a lot of sales that would otherwise slip away.

With Uppush’s built-in email tools and automations, it’s easy to follow up, build trust, and turn first-time visitors into paying customers — automatically.
