Paid ads can bring new visitors to your Shopify store, but they do not have to be the only way to grow your email list. In fact, many stores already have an audience they are not fully capturing. Visitors browse products, read content, check prices, or start checkout — then leave without giving the store a way to reach them again.

The better approach is to **turn more of your existing traffic, customers, and brand interactions into email subscribers**. This gives you an audience you can nurture without paying for another click every time you want to reach them.

* * *

## Why your email list matters more than another ad click

Paid advertising is useful for bringing people to your store, but the relationship often ends when the ad stops running. Email is different. Once a shopper gives you permission to contact them, you can continue communicating through welcome emails, product education, promotions, back-in-stock alerts, and other automated messages.

Popupsmart describes email as an **owned growth channel** because you control the audience, timing, messaging, and segmentation rather than depending entirely on an advertising or social platform.

That does not mean you should stop advertising. It means you should make more of the traffic you already paid for — and the organic traffic you already have — before spending more to acquire new visitors.

* * *

## 10 ways to grow your Shopify email list without increasing ad spend

### 1\. Give visitors a real reason to subscribe

The biggest mistake in email list building is asking for an email without explaining why someone should give it to you.

A generic:

> **Subscribe to our newsletter**

does not offer much value.

Instead, give visitors something specific they can get by subscribing:

-   A first-order discount
-   Free shipping
-   Early access to new products
-   A useful guide or checklist
-   Product recommendations
-   Back-in-stock alerts
-   Exclusive content
-   Members-only offers

Popupsmart emphasizes that the strongest incentives provide **ongoing value**, rather than creating a quick signup that disappears after the first email.

### Try this

Instead of:

> Join our newsletter.

Use:

> **Get early access to new drops**  
> Join our list for new product launches, restocks, and exclusive offers.

The second version gives visitors a reason to stay subscribed, not just a reason to enter their email once.

* * *

### 2\. Optimize your popup instead of adding more traffic

You do not necessarily need more visitors if your current visitors are leaving without subscribing. A well-timed popup can turn part of that existing traffic into an audience you can reach again.

But timing matters. Popupsmart recommends treating email capture as a system based on **timing, relevance, user experience, and behavioral targeting**, rather than showing the same popup to everyone.

#### Test different triggers

-   **Time delay:** Show the popup after visitors have had time to browse.
-   **Scroll depth:** Trigger it after someone shows engagement with the page.
-   **Exit intent:** Offer a reason to stay connected before they leave.
-   **Product behavior:** Show a relevant offer after someone views a product.
-   **Returning visitors:** Use a different message for people who already know your brand.

The goal is not to show more popups.

**The goal is to make each popup more relevant.**

* * *

### 3\. Use different offers for different shoppers

Not every visitor needs the same reason to subscribe. A first-time visitor may want to understand your brand. Someone looking at a product may need reassurance. An existing customer may care more about early access or loyalty benefits. Popupsmart recommends aligning incentives with the visitor’s **lifecycle stage and intent**.

| Visitor | Better signup offer |
| --- | --- |
| First-time visitor | Welcome discount or starter guide |
| Product browser | Product guide or related recommendations |
| High-intent shopper | Shipping information or purchase incentive |
| Returning visitor | New arrivals or early access |
| Existing customer | VIP perks or loyalty benefits |

This can produce a smaller but **more relevant email list** than giving everyone the same discount.

* * *

### 4\. Add signup opportunities beyond your homepage

Your homepage popup should not be the only place where visitors can join your list. Some of the strongest signup opportunities happen when shoppers are already engaged with your store.

Useful locations include:

-   Product pages
-   Collection pages
-   Blog posts
-   Account creation
-   Checkout and notification preferences
-   Order tracking pages
-   Post-purchase pages
-   Loyalty or membership areas

Popupsmart highlights these high-intent surfaces because customers already expect communication in these contexts.

### Example

On an order-tracking page, instead of simply showing delivery information, you could add:

> **Want product care tips and delivery updates?**  
> Get helpful updates by email.

The signup feels natural because it is connected to something the customer is already doing.

* * *

### 5\. Turn your content into a reason to subscribe

You do not always need to offer a discount. Useful content can be a strong lead magnet, especially when your products require education or comparison. Forge Digital Marketing recommends using resources such as **quizzes, guides, checklists, and VIP access** to attract subscribers before asking them to buy.

#### Examples

**Skincare store**

> Download our beginner’s skincare routine guide.

**Fashion store**

> Get our capsule wardrobe checklist.

**Fitness store**

> Take the quiz to find your ideal training routine.

**Home products**

> Get our complete product care guide.

The key is to choose something that solves a problem related to what you sell.

* * *

### 6\. Use quizzes and interactive experiences

A quiz can do two jobs at the same time:

**Capture the email + learn what the shopper wants.**

For example:

> **Find your perfect skincare routine**  
> Answer 5 quick questions and get your personalized recommendations.

The visitor receives something useful, while the store learns about their preferences. This information can later be used for segmentation and more relevant email campaigns.

Forge Digital Marketing specifically highlights quizzes such as product or routine finders as useful lead magnets, while 1Digital identifies **gamified popups** as another way Shopify stores can encourage visitors to subscribe.

* * *

### 7\. Build an email list from customers you already have

Your existing customers are an important source of future subscribers. They already know your brand and have completed a purchase, so you can create relevant opportunities to keep the relationship going.

Consider email signup opportunities around:

-   Account creation
-   Order updates
-   Product registration
-   Loyalty programs
-   Product care information
-   Post-purchase content
-   Reorder reminders

For example:

> **Get more from your purchase**  
> Join our email list for product tips, care guides, and early access to new releases.

This is different from trying to convince a completely new visitor to trust your brand.

* * *

### 8\. Use back-in-stock and product alerts as signup opportunities

Sometimes the strongest reason to subscribe is **a product the shopper already wants**. If an item is out of stock, let visitors request an email notification when it becomes available again.

The same principle can work for:

-   Back-in-stock alerts
-   Price-drop alerts
-   New product alerts
-   Restock updates
-   Product launch notifications

The advantage is intent. Someone asking to be notified about a specific product has already shown interest. You are not simply collecting an email address — you are capturing a **behavioral signal** that can be used later.

* * *

### 9\. Turn social media traffic into subscribers

Social media can send people to your Shopify store, but you do not want the relationship to end with an Instagram or TikTok visit. Instead of constantly asking followers to “shop now,” give them another reason to join your email list.

#### For example:

**Instagram**

> Want first access to our next drop?  
> Join the list through the link in bio.

**TikTok**

> Get the full product guide and exclusive launch updates by email.

**Pinterest**

> Download the free guide and get new collection updates.

This turns social traffic into an audience you can reach later without depending on another algorithm or paid campaign.

* * *

### 10\. Partner with complementary brands

You can also grow your list by reaching people who are already interested in your product category. Forge Digital Marketing lists **partnerships and giveaways with complementary brands** among the tactics that can support broader email list growth.

For example:

-   A skincare brand + makeup brand
-   A coffee brand + mug company
-   A fitness apparel brand + fitness equipment store
-   A pet food brand + pet accessory brand

The key is choosing a partner whose audience overlaps with yours without directly competing with you. A joint guide, giveaway, product bundle, or special event can give both brands access to a relevant new audience.

## Don’t make discounts your only strategy

Discounts are effective because they provide an immediate reason to subscribe. But relying on them for every signup can create a list full of people who only engage when there is a coupon.

Popupsmart makes a useful distinction between **discount-driven** and **value-driven** list growth. Discount offers can generate faster signup volume, while content, early access, loyalty benefits, and other forms of ongoing value can create stronger long-term relationships.

### Instead of always saying:

> Get 10% off.

Test alternatives such as:

> **Get first access to new collections.**

> **Join our product care club.**

> **Get weekly tips and inspiration.**

> **Be the first to know when your favorites are back.**

You can even test discount and non-discount offers against each other. The goal is to find what attracts **valuable subscribers**, not simply the most subscribers.

* * *

## Make your signup promise clear

A good email signup should tell people what they are agreeing to receive.

Avoid vague promises such as:

> Get updates from us.

Instead, be specific:

> **Join 20,000+ shoppers for new product launches, useful tips, and exclusive offers.**

Or:

> **Get 1–2 emails a week with product guides, new arrivals, and member-only offers.**

Popupsmart recommends setting expectations around **content, frequency, value, and user choice** from the first interaction. This can help create a healthier relationship between your store and subscribers.

* * *

## Keep the signup process simple

More fields usually mean more work for the visitor.

For most Shopify stores, the starting point can be as simple as:

**Email address → CTA → Confirmation**

If you need more information, collect it progressively.

For example:

**First interaction:**  
Email address

**Later:**  
Product preferences

**After purchase:**  
Purchase behavior

**Over time:**  
Interests and engagement

Popupsmart recommends progressive profile building rather than asking visitors for everything at once.

* * *

## Your welcome email matters as much as the signup

Getting someone onto your list is only the first step. If the first email is irrelevant, overly promotional, or never arrives, the signup loses much of its value.

A simple welcome flow can:

1.  **Deliver the promised incentive or resource**
2.  **Introduce your brand**
3.  **Explain what makes your products different**
4.  **Share useful content or social proof**
5.  **Guide the subscriber toward a relevant product**

Forge Digital Marketing recommends delivering a lead magnet immediately and then moving subscribers into an existing welcome flow. This is where an email list starts becoming a revenue channel rather than simply a database of addresses.

* * *

## Measure list quality, not just list size

A growing subscriber count looks good, but it does not tell you whether your list is healthy.

Track metrics such as:

| Metric | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| **Signup rate** | Shows how well your capture points convert |
| **Engagement rate** | Shows whether subscribers care about your emails |
| **Click rate** | Shows whether content drives action |
| **Purchase rate** | Connects list growth to revenue |
| **Unsubscribe rate** | Reveals poor expectations or irrelevant content |
| **Revenue per subscriber** | Shows the commercial value of your list |

Popupsmart recommends treating KPIs as **behavioral signals**, rather than simply using them as a scoreboard. A list of 10,000 engaged subscribers can be much more valuable than a list of 50,000 people who never open an email.

## A simple 30-day plan for Shopify stores

You do not need to implement every strategy at once. Start with the parts that can use your existing traffic.

### Week 1: Fix the foundation

-   Create one clear signup offer.
-   Make the form mobile-friendly.
-   Remove unnecessary fields.
-   Set clear expectations.
-   Make sure subscribers enter the correct email flow.

### Week 2: Improve your capture points

Add or improve signup opportunities on:

-   Homepage
-   Product pages
-   Blog
-   Checkout/account areas
-   Post-purchase pages

### Week 3: Add a stronger reason to subscribe

Test a second offer such as:

-   Guide
-   Quiz
-   Early access
-   Back-in-stock alerts
-   Product recommendations
-   Gamified signup

### Week 4: Review the data

Compare:

-   Signup rate
-   Subscriber engagement
-   Purchases
-   Unsubscribes
-   Revenue per subscriber

Then keep the campaigns that attract the **best subscribers**, not simply the most subscribers.

* * *

## How to grow your email list without spending more on ads: the key idea

You do not need to choose between **paid acquisition** and **email list growth**. The smarter approach is to make your existing acquisition work harder. A visitor who comes from Google, social media, an organic search result, a referral, or an existing customer relationship should have a clear opportunity to become a subscriber when the timing makes sense.

That means building a system:

**Traffic → Relevant signup opportunity → Welcome flow → Useful content → Personalized offers → Purchase → Retention**

Forge Digital Marketing describes this kind of value-first approach as a way to turn subscribers into a warmer audience before asking them to buy.

* * *

## Final thoughts

Growing a Shopify email list does not have to mean spending more money to bring more people to your store.

Start by **capturing more value from the audience you already have**. Use better popups, high-intent signup points, useful lead magnets, product alerts, social CTAs, partnerships, and post-purchase opportunities. Most importantly, give people a reason to stay subscribed after the initial signup.

The goal is not simply:

**“How can I collect more emails?”**

It is:

**“How can I turn more of my existing audience into people who want to hear from my brand?”**

That shift makes email list growth more sustainable — and reduces how much you need to rely on continuously paying for the next visitor.
