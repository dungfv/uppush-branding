Klaviyo and Omnisend are two of the most popular email marketing platforms for Shopify. Both offer ecommerce automation, segmentation, customer data, and integrations designed to help stores recover sales and increase repeat purchases. But they are built around slightly different priorities.

**Klaviyo focuses more heavily on customer data, advanced segmentation, predictive analytics, and customization. Omnisend focuses more on ease of use, ecommerce automation, and combining email with SMS and web push.**

So which one is better for Shopify?

There is no universal winner. The better choice depends on your **store size, marketing team, automation needs, budget, and how deeply you want to use customer data**.

* * *

## Klaviyo vs Omnisend at a glance

Before comparing individual features, here is the short version:

|  | **Klaviyo** | **Omnisend** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Best for | Data-driven, growing ecommerce brands | Small to mid-sized Shopify stores |
| Shopify integration | Excellent | Excellent |
| Segmentation | **More advanced** | Strong and easier to use |
| Predictive analytics | **Yes** | More limited |
| Automation | Highly customizable | **Fast to set up** |
| Email | Excellent | Excellent |
| SMS | Available, separate pricing | **Strong multi-channel integration** |
| Web push | No | **Yes** |
| Ease of use | More complex | **Easier** |
| Reporting | **Deeper analytics** | Strong ecommerce reporting |
| Pricing | Generally higher | **Generally lower** |
| Best advantage | Data depth | Value + simplicity |

The sources broadly agree on the main distinction: **Klaviyo has more depth, while Omnisend is easier to use and often more cost-efficient for smaller stores.**

* * *

## 1\. Shopify integration

Both platforms connect directly with Shopify, so you can build campaigns around products, customers, orders, browsing behavior, and cart activity. The difference is in **how deeply each platform uses that data**.

### Klaviyo

Klaviyo is particularly strong at bringing Shopify customer data into its segmentation and automation system. It can work with purchase history, browsing behavior, cart activity, product information, and other customer properties. This gives advanced users more flexibility when building highly specific audiences.

For example, you can create segments around customers who:

-   Purchased a specific product
-   Spent more than a certain amount
-   Viewed a product but did not purchase
-   Have not purchased recently
-   Are predicted to purchase again soon

### Omnisend

Omnisend also has a strong Shopify integration and is built specifically around ecommerce use cases. Its product picker, cart recovery features, purchase data, and pre-built workflows make it relatively quick to turn Shopify data into campaigns.

**Verdict:** Both are strong. **Klaviyo has the edge for deeper data use, while Omnisend is easier to get running quickly.**

* * *

## 2\. Segmentation and customer data

This is one of the biggest differences between the two platforms. Klaviyo’s segmentation can combine **behavioral, profile, purchase, and predictive data**. Its predictive capabilities include metrics such as predicted lifetime value, churn risk, and expected next purchase date.

This becomes useful when a store has a large amount of customer data and wants to make more advanced decisions.

For example:

> Customers who spent over $200, purchased in the last 90 days, have high predicted lifetime value, and have not engaged with an email recently.

Omnisend handles the more common ecommerce segmentation needs well. It also offers a natural-language segment builder, allowing merchants to describe an audience instead of manually creating every condition.

### Choose Klaviyo if:

-   You need complex customer segments.
-   You have a large customer database.
-   Predictive analytics matter to your strategy.
-   You have someone who can manage advanced segmentation.

### Choose Omnisend if:

-   You mainly need purchase and engagement segments.
-   You want to create segments quickly.
-   You prefer simpler tools.
-   Your store does not need predictive customer scoring.

**Winner for advanced segmentation: Klaviyo.**

**Winner for simplicity: Omnisend.**

* * *

## 3\. Email automation

Both platforms support the core Shopify automations most stores need.

These include:

-   Welcome series
-   Abandoned cart
-   Browse abandonment
-   Post-purchase
-   Win-back
-   Product-related campaigns

Omnisend puts a strong emphasis on **ready-made ecommerce workflows**. Its pre-built automations can help a store launch common campaigns with less setup. Klaviyo offers more room for customization. You can build more complex branches and use detailed customer data to decide what happens next in a flow.

For example, a Klaviyo flow could behave differently depending on:

**Customer value → Product category → Purchase history → Engagement → Predicted behavior**

That flexibility is powerful, but it also creates a steeper learning curve.

**Verdict:**  
**Omnisend** is better when you want to launch standard ecommerce automations quickly.

**Klaviyo** is better when you want to build highly customized customer journeys.

* * *

## 4\. Email campaigns and design

Both platforms provide drag-and-drop email builders, templates, product blocks, and ecommerce-focused content. Klaviyo is particularly strong at **dynamic product personalization**. Its product feeds can be used to recommend products based on browsing and customer behavior.

Omnisend also makes adding products to emails simple through its product picker and product recommendation tools. The difference is less about whether you can create a good email and more about how much personalization you want behind it.

### Klaviyo makes more sense if:

You want emails to adapt heavily to individual customer behavior.

### Omnisend makes more sense if:

You want to create attractive ecommerce emails quickly without spending as much time configuring them.

**Verdict: Close. Klaviyo has more personalization depth, while Omnisend emphasizes ease of use.**

* * *

## 5\. SMS and web push

This is an important difference for Shopify stores building multi-channel marketing. Omnisend supports **email, SMS, and web push** within its broader marketing platform and workflows. This allows a store to combine different channels in one customer journey.

For example:

> Email sent → customer doesn’t engage → SMS reminder → web push notification

Klaviyo also supports SMS, but it does **not offer native web push**. Its SMS is managed separately from the core email pricing structure. This matters if web push is an important part of your strategy.

### If you mainly need email:

Both platforms can work well.

### If you want email + SMS + web push:

**Omnisend has the broader native channel mix.**

* * *

## 6\. Reporting and analytics

Both platforms provide campaign and automation reporting, but they approach analytics differently. Klaviyo stands out for its **predictive analytics and deeper customer intelligence**. It can use predictive metrics such as expected lifetime value, churn risk, and expected next purchase date.

This can help larger brands make decisions such as:

-   Which customers deserve VIP treatment?
-   Who is likely to churn?
-   Who may purchase again soon?
-   Who should receive an incentive?
-   Which segments are becoming less engaged?

Omnisend focuses more on practical ecommerce reporting and revenue attribution. Venture Harbour’s 2026 comparison found Omnisend stronger in granular revenue attribution, while Klaviyo had the advantage in predictive reporting and benchmarking.

**Verdict:**  
For **predictive customer analytics**, Klaviyo has the advantage.

For **straightforward ecommerce reporting**, Omnisend is often enough.

* * *

## 7\. Ease of use

This is where the difference can become obvious for smaller teams. Omnisend is generally positioned as the easier platform to learn. Its interface and pre-built workflows allow merchants to get common ecommerce campaigns running relatively quickly. Klaviyo gives you more options, but that also means there is more to learn.

A small Shopify store with one person handling marketing may not need dozens of segmentation conditions or predictive models. A larger ecommerce team may see that complexity as an advantage.

### Think about it this way:

**Omnisend:**

> “Give me the tools I need to get my ecommerce marketing running.”

**Klaviyo:**

> “Give me deeper control over every part of my customer data.”

Neither approach is inherently better. It depends on how much complexity your team can actually use.

* * *

## 8\. Pricing

Pricing changes regularly, so exact numbers should always be checked before choosing a platform. The five sources consistently describe **Omnisend as the lower-cost option in many comparable list-size scenarios**, although the exact gap varies depending on plan, contacts, and channels.

For example, one 2026 comparison lists approximate monthly pricing at:

| Contacts | Klaviyo | Omnisend |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 500 | ~$20 | ~$16 |
| 5,000 | ~$100 | ~$81 |
| 10,000 | ~$150 | ~$132 |
| 25,000 | ~$375 | ~$259 |

These figures come from third-party comparisons and should be treated as **reference points, not permanent pricing**. Another 2026 analysis makes a similar argument: Omnisend can provide better price-to-feature value for smaller DTC brands, while Klaviyo’s additional data capabilities may become more valuable as a store grows.

### The important question is not:

> Which platform is cheaper?

It is:

> **Will I actually use the features I’m paying for?**

Paying more for advanced predictive analytics makes sense only if those capabilities contribute enough value to justify the extra cost.

* * *

## 9\. Which one is better for different Shopify stores?

There is no single answer for every store.

### Choose Klaviyo if you:

-   Run a **larger or fast-growing Shopify store**.
-   Have a dedicated marketing or CRM team.
-   Need advanced segmentation.
-   Want predictive customer analytics.
-   Manage complex automation journeys.
-   Have the resources to configure and maintain a more advanced platform.
-   Want a large ecosystem of integrations and agencies.

The sources particularly point toward Klaviyo for more mature, data-driven ecommerce operations.

### Choose Omnisend if you:

-   Run a **small or mid-sized Shopify store**.
-   Want to get started quickly.
-   Prefer simpler automation.
-   Want email and SMS together.
-   Want web push as part of your channel mix.
-   Care strongly about price-to-feature value.
-   Do not need advanced predictive analytics.

Omnisend’s main advantage is not that it has more features than Klaviyo. It is that **many of the features a typical ecommerce store needs are easier to access without as much complexity**.

* * *

## 10\. What about Shopify Plus?

Shopify Plus merchants often have more complex customer data, larger lists, and more sophisticated marketing requirements. This is where Klaviyo’s additional depth can become more valuable.

Venture Harbour notes that Klaviyo’s deeper Shopify Plus capabilities, predictive analytics, reporting, and segmentation can justify its higher cost for larger stores with dedicated marketing teams. That does not mean every Shopify Plus store needs Klaviyo.

The right question remains:

**Are you actually using those advanced capabilities?**

If your marketing strategy is still mainly welcome emails, abandoned carts, promotions, and post-purchase campaigns, a simpler platform may be sufficient.

* * *

## 11\. Klaviyo vs Omnisend: A practical decision guide

If you are still unsure, use this quick framework.

### Pick Klaviyo when:

**Data complexity > simplicity**

You have enough customer data and marketing resources to benefit from advanced segmentation and predictive analytics.

### Pick Omnisend when:

**Simplicity + value > advanced analytics**

You want strong ecommerce automation without building a highly complex marketing infrastructure.

### Consider another platform when:

**Neither platform matches your actual channel strategy.**

For example, if your main priorities are **email + web push + Shopify automation** rather than predictive analytics or a large enterprise ecosystem, there are other options worth considering.

* * *

## 12\. What about Uppush as a Klaviyo alternative?

For Shopify merchants comparing Klaviyo and Omnisend, **Uppush is another option to consider**, particularly if your priorities are email, web push, ecommerce automation, and flexible usage-based pricing.

Uppush combines **email marketing and web push** in one Shopify-focused platform. It includes automation for abandoned carts, back-in-stock, price drops, browse abandonment, product releases, shipping notifications, and customer win-back campaigns depending on the plan.

One major difference is its pricing model.

Uppush offers a **free plan with unlimited subscribers**, while paid usage is based on email and web push volume rather than simply charging more as your contact database grows. The current Pro plan starts at $12.99/month and includes additional email and web push allowances.

This can be interesting for stores that have a large subscriber base but do not want their software bill to increase simply because their contact list grows.

### Uppush may be worth considering if you want:

-   **Email + web push in one platform**
-   Shopify-focused automation
-   Abandoned cart and checkout recovery
-   Browse abandonment
-   Price-drop alerts
-   Back-in-stock notifications
-   Customer segmentation
-   Popup and spin-wheel subscriber collection
-   Usage-based pricing
-   Unlimited subscribers on the free plan

Uppush also supports event-based web push automation, allowing stores to combine email and push around behaviors such as cart abandonment, browse abandonment, price drops, back-in-stock events, and welcome journeys.

The trade-off is that **Uppush is not positioned as a direct replacement for every advanced Klaviyo capability**. If predictive customer analytics, highly complex data models, or enterprise-level reporting are your main requirements, Klaviyo may remain the better fit.

But if your priority is **practical Shopify marketing automation without paying for complexity you do not need**, it is worth putting Uppush on the shortlist.

## Common mistakes when choosing between them

### Choosing the most powerful platform by default

More features do not automatically mean better results. If your team only uses five automation flows, paying for advanced predictive capabilities may not create enough additional value to justify the complexity.

### Choosing based only on price

The cheapest platform is not always the cheapest overall. Consider the time required to build campaigns, manage segments, maintain automations, and analyze results.

### Ignoring your channel strategy

If you want email, SMS, and web push, compare the **complete channel experience**, not just email features.

### Comparing today’s needs with tomorrow’s hypothetical needs

A small store does not necessarily need an enterprise-level marketing platform because it plans to grow. Choose the platform that makes sense for your **current stage**, then reassess when your requirements change.

* * *

## Final verdict: Which is better for Shopify?

**Klaviyo** is better for Shopify stores that need advanced customer data, predictive analytics, complex segmentation, and highly customizable marketing automation.

**Omnisend** is better for Shopify stores that want strong ecommerce automation, simpler setup, broader native channels, and generally lower costs.

And **Uppush** is worth considering if your priority is Shopify-focused email + web push automation, especially when unlimited subscribers and usage-based pricing are attractive.

So the decision can be simplified:

> Need maximum data depth? → Klaviyo  
> Need simple multi-channel ecommerce marketing? → Omnisend  
> Need email + web push with flexible usage-based pricing? → Uppush

There is no reason to choose the most expensive or most advanced platform simply because it is popular.

The better choice is the one that gives your Shopify store **the capabilities you will actually use, at a cost and complexity your team can sustain.**
