In the world of email marketing, getting your emails _opened_ is just the first step. The real success lies in getting readers to _take action_—whether that’s making a purchase, signing up for something, or simply clicking through. One key factor that directly affects this? **Domain verification.**

## What Is Domain Verification?

Domain verification is the process of proving that you own the domain you’re sending emails from. Platforms like Google and Yahoo are now making this a _requirement_, not just a best practice. It usually involves adding DNS records to your domain (like SPF, DKIM, and DMARC) to confirm your identity as a sender.

## Why It’s Important

1.  **Inbox Placement**  
    Verified domains are more likely to land in the inbox—not the spam folder. That means more people actually _see_ your emails.
2.  **Brand Trust**  
    When recipients see your real domain (like `you@yourstore.com`) instead of a generic email, it builds credibility. That trust directly affects whether someone clicks or buys.
3.  **Improved Conversion Rates**  
    Better deliverability and higher trust mean better engagement. We’ve seen stores using Uppush see **10–20% improvements in conversion rates** after verifying their domain and cleaning up their sender reputation.

One Uppush user running a mid-sized Shopify store had consistently low open and click-through rates. After verifying their sending domain and adjusting a few DNS settings, they saw:
