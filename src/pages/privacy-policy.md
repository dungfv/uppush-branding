## Summary of key points

_This summary highlights the key points of this Privacy Policy. It is provided for convenience only and does not replace the full Privacy Policy below. You can find more detailed explanations by referring to the relevant sections in this document._

**What personal data do we process?** When Merchants install and use the Uppush application, and when End Users interact with marketing campaigns created via Uppush, we process personal data necessary to provide marketing automation services. This may include contact details, order and cart data, engagement data, and technical usage information, depending on how the Services are used.

**Do we process sensitive personal data?** No. Uppush does not intentionally collect or process special categories of personal data as defined under GDPR (such as health data, biometric data, or religious beliefs).

**Do we receive personal data from third parties?** Yes, in a limited and controlled manner. We primarily receive End User data from Shopify and merchant-configured opt-in sources (such as pop-ups or forms) solely to deliver the Services. We do not purchase personal data from data brokers.

**How do we use personal data?** We process personal data to operate, provide, and improve our marketing automation Services, including campaign delivery, personalization, analytics, security, fraud prevention, and legal compliance. We process personal data only when we have a valid legal basis under applicable data protection laws.

**When and with whom do we share personal data?** We do not sell personal data. We may share personal data with carefully selected sub-processors (such as hosting, messaging delivery, and analytics providers) only where necessary to provide the Services and subject to strict contractual data protection obligations.

**How do we keep personal data secure?** We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect personal data. However, no system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security against unauthorized access or misuse.

**What rights do individuals have?** Depending on applicable law (such as GDPR, UK GDPR, or CCPA), individuals may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of their personal data, as well as to withdraw consent for marketing communications.

**How can these rights be exercised?** End Users should primarily submit requests through the relevant Merchant, who acts as the Data Controller. Merchants may also contact Uppush for assistance. We respond to all valid requests in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

_To learn more about how Uppush collects, uses, and protects personal data, please review the full Privacy Policy below._

## 1. Introduction and scope

This Privacy Notice explains how **Uppush** (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and discloses information in connection with the Uppush marketing automation application for **Shopify**.

_**This policy applies to:**_

**Merchants:** Any business or legal entity that installs and uses the Uppush application on their Shopify store.

**Merchants’ Customers:** Individuals who interact with a Merchant’s store and marketing assets created via Uppush (collectively referred to as “End Users”).

**Under applicable data protection laws:**

-   Merchants act as the Data Controller with respect to End User personal data.
-   Uppush acts as a Data Processor, processing personal data solely on the documented instructions of the Merchant.

_This Privacy Policy is designed to comply with global data protection standards, including the GDPR (EU), UK GDPR, and, where applicable, the CCPA._

## 2. Information we collect

_In the context of marketing automation, we collect and process data through the following categories._

### 2.1 Information from Merchants

-   **Account Information:** Name, business email address, company name, billing address, and payment-related metadata.
-   **Store Information:** Shopify store domain, store currency, Shopify plan type, and app configuration settings.
-   **Support Communications:** Information provided when Merchants contact our support team.

### 2.2 Information about End Users (Customer Data)

_To provide marketing application  features, Uppush synchronizes data from Shopify and merchant-configured opt-in sources, which may include:_

**Identifiers:** Name, email address, phone number (if provided), IP address, device identifiers, and Shopify customer IDs.

**Commercial Information:** Order history, cart contents, abandoned checkout data, products viewed, purchase frequency, and lifetime value (LTV).

**Engagement Data:** Interactions with emails, web push notifications, pop-ups, and campaigns (such as opens, clicks, and conversions).

_Uppush does not intentionally collect special categories of personal data as defined under GDPR._

### 2.3 Automatically Collected Information

**Usage and Log Data:** IP addresses, browser type, operating system, device information, timestamps, and feature usage data, collected for security, performance monitoring, and system optimization.

## 3. How we use information?

_We process personal data exclusively to deliver and improve specialized marketing automation services, including:_

**Marketing Automation:** Enabling workflows such as abandoned cart recovery, welcome messages, post-purchase follow-ups, re-engagement campaigns, and automated email marketing sequences.

**Email Marketing Campaigns:** Creating, sending, and optimizing email marketing campaigns on behalf of Merchants, including newsletters, promotional emails, lifecycle emails, and transactional-related communications where applicable.

**Personalization and Segmentation:** Creating audience segments based on shopping behavior, engagement patterns, or merchant-defined criteria to deliver relevant content.

**Analytics and Attribution:** Measuring campaign performance, conversions, and return on investment (ROI).

**Service Improvement:** Using aggregated and de-identified data to enhance platform performance, reliability, and predictive marketing capabilities.

## 4. Global data transfers and GDPR compliance

### 4.1 International Data Transfers

Uppush operates on a global infrastructure. Personal data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside the European Economic Area (EEA), including the United States, where our sub-processors and infrastructure providers are located.

### 4.2 EU–US Data Privacy Framework

Where applicable, Uppush relies on the **EU–US Data Privacy Framework**, the UK Extension to the **EU–US Data Privacy Framework**, and the Swiss–US Data Privacy Framework, as administered by the U.S. Department of Commerce. These frameworks ensure lawful and secure transfers of personal data from the EEA, UK, and Switzerland to the United States

### 4.3 Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)

For transfers not covered by an adequacy decision, we rely on **Standard Contractual Clauses** (pursuant to Art. 46(2) and (3) GDPR). These are model templates provided by the EU Commission (specifically **Decision 2021/914**) to ensure data protection standards remain consistent across borders.

-   You can view the specific SCC framework here: [_EUR-Lex 32021D0914_](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dec_impl/2021/914/oj).

## 6. Data retention and deletion

-   **Retention Period:** We retain personal data as long as the Merchant maintains an active account with us.
    
-   **App Uninstallation:** Upon uninstallation from a Shopify store, we initiate a data deletion process for all synced Customer Data within 48 hours, in accordance with Shopify’s Mandatory Webhooks. _While active data is removed from our production systems during this window, residual encrypted data may remain in our secure system backups and technical logs for up to 30 days, after which it is fully purged. During this period, the data is isolated from any further processing._
    
-   **Exceptions:** We may retain certain data as required by law or for legitimate business purposes (e.g., tax records or fraud prevention).
    

## 7. Data subject rights

Under GDPR, UK GDPR, and applicable privacy laws, End Users have the right to:

**Access and Portability:** Request access to personal data and receive a copy.

**Rectification:** Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.

**Erasure:** Request deletion of personal data (“right to be forgotten”).

**Restriction or Objection:** Limit or object to certain processing activities.

**Withdrawal of Consent:** Opt out of marketing communications at any time.

_Merchants are responsible for managing and responding to End User requests as Data Controllers. Uppush supports Merchants in fulfilling these requests where required._

## 8. Security measures

Uppush applies Privacy by Design and Privacy by Default principles and implements appropriate technical and organizational safeguards, including:

**Encryption:** Industry-standard encryption for data in transit and at rest.

**Access Controls:** Role-based access controls and authentication mechanisms for internal systems.

**Monitoring and Testing:** Continuous monitoring, logging, and regular security reviews to protect against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse of data.

## 9. Controls for do-not-track features

Most web browsers and some mobile operating systems and mobile applications include a Do-Not-Track (“DNT”) feature or setting you can activate to signal your privacy preference not to have data about your online browsing activities monitored and collected. At this stage no uniform technology standard for recognizing and implementing DNT signals has been finalized. As such, we do not currently respond to DNT browser signals or any other mechanism that automatically communicates your choice not to be tracked online. If a standard for online tracking is adopted that we must follow in the future, we will inform you about that practice in a revised version of this privacy notice.
